# Terravision vs Google Earth — IPR Case Study

This repository contains a comprehensive case study of the intellectual property (IPR) dispute between **ART+COM** (Terravision) and **Google** (Google Earth).

---

## 📖 Overview

- **Case Name:** ART+COM Innovationpool GmbH vs Google LLC  
- **Patent in Dispute:** U.S. Patent **RE44,550**, titled *“Method and Device for Pictorial Representation of Space-Related Data”* :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}  
- **Claim:** ART+COM alleged Google Earth infringed on their patented Terravision technology. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}  
- **Result:** Jury verdict in May 2016 found for Google (non-infringement + patent invalidity). :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}  
- **Appeal:** The **Court of Appeals (Federal Circuit)** affirmed that SRI’s earlier TerraVision was prior art, invalidating ART+COM’s patent. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
